WELCOME TO JOBPULSE AI!
This manual provides a detailed walkthrough of all features in the Job Search Portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXPLORE JOBS TAB (MAIN FEED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- GLOBAL SEARCH BAR: Enter any job title (e.g. "Software Engineer", "Product Designer"),
  tech stack keyword ("Python", "React", "AWS"), or company name ("ByteDance", "Google").
- LOCATION FILTER: Enter city, state, or "Remote" to filter listings geographically.
- PLATFORM SELECTOR: Toggle job sources between Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor.
- JOB TYPE SELECTOR: Filter by Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, or Internship.
- WORKPLACE FILTER: Instantly isolate Remote-Only roles vs On-Site/Hybrid roles.
- SORTING: Sort listings by Newest Posted, Highest Salary, or Title A-Z.
- QUICK PRESET TAGS: Click quick chips like '🚀 Remote Engineer' or '💰 $100k+ Salary'.
- VIEW MODE TOGGLE: Switch between Grid Cards View and Compact Table View.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. JOB DETAILS & DIRECT APPLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Click 'View Details' on any job card to open the complete Job Details Modal.
- View salary breakdowns, contact email, posting age, and workplace type.
- SKILLS TAGS: Automatically extracted skill tags (Python, React, SQL, Docker, etc.).
- FULL DESCRIPTION: Scroll through the raw formatted job description.
- DIRECT APPLY BUTTON: Click 'Apply Directly on Platform' to open the original posting 
  URL on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- KPI METRICS: Real-time calculation of total active listings, average annual salary, 
  remote ratio percentage, and top scraped job board.
- SALARY DISTRIBUTION CHART: Visual bar graph of salary ranges ($50k-$80k up to $170k+).
- PLATFORM BREAKDOWN: Donut chart showing distribution across LinkedIn vs Indeed vs ZipRecruiter.
- TOP IN-DEMAND SKILLS: Horizontal bar chart showing the top 10 most requested skills.
- WORKPLACE RATIO: Pie chart comparing Remote vs On-Site opportunities.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAVED APPLICATIONS TRACKER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BOOKMARKING: Click the bookmark ribbon on any job card to save it.
- APPLICATION PIPELINE STAGES: Categorize applications into:
  * 📌 Saved
  * 📩 Applied
  * 🎯 Interviewing
  * 🎉 Offered
- PRIVATE NOTES: Add custom notes (e.g., "Interviewer name: John, follow up on Friday").
- PERSISTENCE: Data automatically persists in browser storage (localStorage).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LIVE SCRAPER CONTROL PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Configure dynamic job scrapers in real time:
  * Search Keyword & Location
  * Results count per site (1-50)
  * Posting age window (Hours old, default 72h)
  * Target site checkboxes (Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor)
  * Remote-only checkbox
- Output Console Terminal displays live progress logs and status feedback.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATASET IMPORT & EXPORT SUITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UPLOAD CSV: Click 'Upload CSV' in top navbar to load any custom job CSV dataset.
- EXPORT DATA: Click 'Export Data' to download the active filtered dataset as a CSV file.
